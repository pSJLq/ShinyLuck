ShinyLuck is a provably-fair casino and poker room on the Somnia testnet. Casino outcomes are drawn by a commit-reveal scheme enforced by the Casino contract; poker cards are dealt by mental poker (zkShuffle) so no server ever sees your hand. This page covers how it all works and where the contracts live.

Overview

Two products share one site, one wallet and one on-chain profile:

Casino · seven games (Dice, Mines, Plinko, Roulette, Crash, VAULT.7, SUGAR.LAB). Every bet is a transaction against the Casino contract; every outcome derives from randomness the contract computes itself. A settlement bot reveals pre-committed seeds and keeps shared rounds (Crash, Roulette) cycling · it holds no power over results.

Poker · full no-limit hold'em, cash tables and tournaments, all chips and actions on-chain in the PokerRoom contract. The deck is shuffled by the players themselves in-browser with zero-knowledge proofs at every step (zkShuffle v2).

Wallet & STT

Sign in with email · Privy issues you a Somnia Global Wallet (no extension needed), the same wallet across every Somnia partner app. Transactions are signed headlessly: no per-bet popups.

The currency is STT, Somnia's testnet token · it carries no monetary value. You need STT in your wallet to cover gas on every on-chain action, so keep a small balance there and play straight from it. Poker uses a separate in-contract bankroll: top it up (and cash out) instantly from the profile drawer.

Casino games

Bets are placed directly from your wallet; wins are paid by the contract. The bet panel shows your live limits · max bet adapts to the house bankroll in real time, and the expected value of every bet is printed next to the payout (the house edge is public per game, read from the contract).

Dice · pick a target, roll over or under. Mines · open safe tiles, each compounds the multiplier; cash out any time. Plinko · a ball drops through the pin field; the path replays the on-chain randomness bits exactly. Roulette · an American wheel with shared rounds: every player is on the same spin. Crash · shared rounds too; cash out before the bust point. VAULT.7 · a 5×3, 20-line slot. SUGAR.LAB · a 7×7 cluster slot with cascades.

Games marked BETA are playable but still rough around the edges.

RNG & settlement

For every bet the contract derives randomness as keccak256(serverSeed ‖ clientSeed ‖ blockhash ‖ nonce):

1 · The house commits keccak256(serverSeed) on-chain before any bet can bind to it.
2 · Your browser contributes a fresh 32-byte client seed with each bet.
3 · After your bet mines, the settlement bot reveals the seed; the contract checks it against the commitment and pays out.
4 · If a reveal is ever missed, the bet auto-refunds after the 256-block window via refundExpired(betId) · callable by anyone.

You can re-derive any outcome in the browser on the Provably Fair page, or from raw chain data.

Poker

Cash tables run 2–9 handed no-limit hold'em. Your table buy-in comes from your PokerRoom balance; every action (bet, call, raise, fold) is an on-chain transaction, and pots settle in the contract.

Cards you can trust: the deck is encrypted and shuffled by the players themselves (mental poker). Each shuffle and each card reveal carries a zero-knowledge proof verified against BN254 · neither other players, nor the coordinator bot, nor the house can see your hole cards before showdown.

Rake is configured per table (hard ceiling 10% of the pot) and capped per hand; the exact rake of each table is shown in the lobby. No flop · no drop.

Tournaments

Anyone can host a tournament. Set the buy-in, blind schedule and seats; players register (with host approval if you want a curated field). Prizes pay out from the pool automatically at the final table.

Host reward · the creator can take a cut of the pool, capped at 10% (set at creation, visible to everyone before registering).
Sponsored prizes · anyone can add STT to a tournament's prize pool; sponsored deposits carry a 10% platform fee.

Profile

One profile for casino and poker, stored on-chain: your nickname lives in the PlayerProfile contract and your avatar image in AvatarStore (yes, the actual image bytes are on-chain). Set both from the profile drawer · they show up at poker tables, in the lobby and on the leaderboard.
