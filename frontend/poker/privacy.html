Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 July 2026 · Testnet release · not legal advice.
Privacy by architecture. ShinyPoker is non-custodial and pseudonymous. We don't ask for your name, email or KYC to play. Your identity on the felt is your wallet address (or an ENS-style name you choose).

1. What we don't collect

  • No real names, emails, phone numbers or government IDs for standard play.
  • No custody of funds · balances live in escrow contracts you control.
  • Opponents never see your hole cards before showdown, and the deal is provably fair (sealed on-chain, verified after). See Provably Fair for exactly how card handling works today.

2. What is inherently public

Because the game is on-chain, certain data is public on Somnia by design: wallet addresses, deposits/withdrawals, buy-ins, betting actions, pots, rake and payouts. Anyone can read these from the block explorer. This is the transparency that makes the game provably fair.

3. What the interface stores locally

  • Preferences (table theme, 4-color deck, sound, animation speed) in your browser's local storage.
  • Session-key authorizations scoped to a table, until you revoke them or leave.

4. Analytics

We may collect anonymized, aggregate usage metrics (e.g. page performance) that are not linked to your wallet identity. We do not sell data.

5. Your control

Disconnect your wallet at any time. Clear local storage to remove preferences. Revoke session keys from Settings. On-chain history cannot be deleted · that is a property of public blockchains.
