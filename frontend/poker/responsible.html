Play responsibly

Responsible Gaming

Poker is entertainment and involves financial risk. Play within your means.
18+ only. Never play with money you can't afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, it's time to stop.

Know the signs

  • Chasing losses, or betting more than you planned.
  • Playing to escape stress, or feeling anxious about your play.
  • Borrowing money or neglecting responsibilities to play.

Tools we provide

  • Deposit limits. Set a cap on how much STT you move into escrow per day or week.
  • Session reminders. Optional time-played reminders while you're at the tables.
  • Self-exclusion. Temporarily or permanently block your wallet from joining tables.
  • Transparent balances. The Cashier always shows your escrow vs in-play funds so you know exactly where you stand.

Get support

If you or someone you know needs help, reach out to a problem-gambling support service in your region. Many offer free, confidential help 24/7. Speaking to someone early makes a difference.

A reminder. The house edge in any rake-based game means most players lose over time. Treat poker as paid entertainment, set limits before you sit down, and walk away when you hit them.
