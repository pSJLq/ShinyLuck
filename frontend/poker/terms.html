Eligibility. ShinyPoker is for users 18+ (or the age of majority where you live) in jurisdictions where on-chain skill-and-chance gaming is permitted. By connecting a wallet you confirm you meet these requirements.

1. The service

ShinyPoker is a decentralized, non-custodial No-Limit Texas Hold'em poker room operating on Somnia testnet, denominated in STT (the Somnia test token). Gameplay logic, escrow, pots and payouts are governed by smart contracts. We provide the interface to those contracts; we do not custody your funds.

2. Your wallet & funds

  • You are solely responsible for your wallet, private keys and session keys. Lost keys cannot be recovered by us.
  • Deposits move STT into an escrow contract you can withdraw from. Withdrawals are processed on-chain.
  • Network (gas) fees apply to on-chain actions and are set by Somnia, not by us.

3. Fair play

Bots, collusion, ghosting, real-time-assistance tools and multi-accounting at the same table are prohibited and may result in forfeiture of in-play funds held under the relevant contract rules and a ban from the interface.

4. Rake & fees

Cash tables charge a transparent per-hand rake with a cap ("no flop, no drop"). Tournaments charge a fee shown as a split of the buy-in (a flat 10% platform fee; the rest builds the prize pool). All fees are displayed before you commit funds.
