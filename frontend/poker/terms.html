Eligibility. ShinyPoker is for users 18+ (or the age of majority where you live) in jurisdictions where on-chain skill-and-chance gaming is permitted. By connecting a wallet you confirm you meet these requirements.

1. The service

ShinyPoker is a decentralized, non-custodial No-Limit Texas Hold'em poker room operating on Somnia mainnet, denominated in SOMI. Gameplay logic, escrow, pots and payouts are governed by smart contracts. We provide the interface to those contracts; we do not custody your funds.

2. Your wallet & funds

  • You are solely responsible for your wallet, private keys and session keys. Lost keys cannot be recovered by us.
  • Deposits move SOMI into an escrow contract you can withdraw from. Withdrawals are processed on-chain.
  • Network (gas) fees apply to on-chain actions and are set by Somnia, not by us.

3. Fair play

Bots, collusion, ghosting, real-time-assistance tools and multi-accounting at the same table are prohibited and may result in forfeiture of in-play funds held under the relevant contract rules and a ban from the interface.

4. Rake & fees

Cash tables charge a transparent per-hand rake with a cap ("no flop, no drop"). Tournaments charge a fee shown as a split of the buy-in (e.g. "20 SOMI = 18.5 prize pool + 1.5 fee"). All fees are displayed before you commit funds.

5. No warranty

The interface is provided "as is". Smart contracts carry inherent risk. Use at your own discretion; never deposit more than you can afford to lose.
