Questions

Good to know

Is my money actually on-chain?+
Yes. Deposits sit in a non-custodial escrow contract, and every buy-in, bet, pot, rake and payout is an on-chain transaction on Somnia. You can verify all of it on the block explorer. The contract has no path for the house to move your balance — only you can withdraw it.
Can the deal be rigged, and can anyone see my cards?+
The deal cannot be rigged: the deck is shuffled by the players’ own browsers (mental poker), every shuffle carries a zero-knowledge proof, and the whole transcript is committed on-chain before the hand starts — no card can be swapped or stacked, and anyone can audit any hand. On card secrecy: your hole cards are encrypted to all players jointly and decrypted only in your browser, from the other players’ proven shares. Nobody else ever holds them — not your opponents, not the public, not even our dealer service. Every reveal (board and showdown) is verified by the contract before it counts.
Do I sign a transaction for every action?+
No. When you sit down you authorize a capped session key once. After that, folds, calls and raises happen instantly with no wallet popup, while still settling on-chain in the background. Revoke the session any time.
How fast are withdrawals?+
Instant. Withdrawing from escrow has zero lockup — your STT is back in your wallet in seconds, for a sub-cent fee.
What’s the rake?+
Shown transparently on every table — a capped percentage of the pot, “no flop no drop”. Tournament fees are displayed as a split up front: buy-in = prize pool + fee, nothing hidden.
